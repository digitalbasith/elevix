Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Workflow

End-to-end procurement automation connecting purchase requests, suppliers, POs, receipts, invoices and payments.

Overview

The Procure-to-Pay workflow digitizes the procurement lifecycle from business requirement to supplier payment, connecting employees, approvers, procurement and finance in one process.

The Challenge

Manual purchase requests, lengthy approvals, fragmented supplier information, invoice matching issues and limited spend visibility created delays and administrative effort.

Our Solution

We implemented an automated P2P workflow covering purchase requisitions, approvals, supplier and quotation management, purchase orders, goods or service receipt, invoice capture, three-way matching, payment approval and reconciliation.

Key Features

  • Purchase requisitions
  • configurable approvals
  • supplier management
  • quotation comparison
  • purchase orders
  • goods/service receipt
  • invoice processing
  • three-way matching
  • exception handling
  • payment approval
  • payment integration
  • reconciliation.

Business Impact

Reduced manual procurement activity, faster approvals, stronger PO control, improved supplier management, fewer invoice errors, better spend visibility and stronger auditability.
