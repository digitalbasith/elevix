Intelligent Ebook Management

Parallel workflows designed to accelerate ebook operations and improve visibility across publishing teams.

Overview

The ebook management solution connects content preparation, review, approvals and operational handoffs through a structured digital workflow.

The Challenge

Sequential handoffs, manual tracking and disconnected communication slowed ebook turnaround and made progress difficult to see.

Our Solution

Elevix created parallel, trackable workflows that allow teams to progress compatible tasks together while keeping ownership and status visible.

Outcome

The streamlined workflow supports faster ebook turnaround, clearer accountability and better coordination across publishing operations.
